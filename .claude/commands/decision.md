# /decision <topic>

Log an architectural or business decision as a lightweight ADR:

1. Ask (or infer from recent conversation) the decision being made, the alternatives considered, and the chosen path.
2. Write `data/decisions/<date>-<slug>.md` with sections: Context, Options Considered, Decision, Consequences.
3. If the decision affects a specific project, add a one-line pointer to it in that project's `data/projects/<project>.md`.
